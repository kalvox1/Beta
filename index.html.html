AI Receptionist — Tester Guide

Thank you for helping test Kalvox, an AI voice receptionist for Deccan Spice restaurant. This guide walks you through what to try and what to look out for.

Known limitations
  1. WhatsApp confirmations: During a reservation, the AI may offer to send you a WhatsApp confirmation. You might hear this offer but not receive the message — that's expected. The WhatsApp channel is still being built and is not live yet. You don't need to flag this as a bug.
Before you call
  1. Call from any phone — mobile or landline.
  2. You will hear a greeting identifying the AI assistant and a recording notice.
  3. Speak naturally after the greeting — no need to wait for a beep.
  4. Try each scenario below in separate calls, or chain a few in one call.
Scenarios to test
1
Ask about today's specials
FAQ
Say something like
"What are today's specials?" or "Do you have any specials tonight?"
Expected: The AI reads out the day's featured dishes with a brief description and price.
2
Make a reservation
RESERVATION
Start with
"I'd like to make a reservation" or "Can I book a table?"
AI asks: party size?
AI asks: preferred date and time?
AI asks: name for the booking?
AI confirms and saves — you hear a confirmation read back.
Tip: State your intent first ("I'd like to make a reservation"), then let the AI guide you step by step.
Name tip: When asked for your name, say it clearly and slowly. If the AI mishears it, try spelling your first name letter by letter — e.g. "R-A-H-U-L". Note down whether it got it right either way.
3
Ask about opening hours
HOURS
Say something like
"Are you open on Sundays?" or "What time do you close?"
Expected: AI gives the restaurant's hours clearly and naturally.
4
Ask a specific question
FAQ
Say something like
"Is the food halal?" or "Do you have parking?" or "Do you do catering?"
Expected: AI answers based on the restaurant's FAQ. Halal, parking, vegetarian options, and catering are all covered. Should sound natural, not robotic.
5
Try to place an order
ORDER
Say something like
"I'd like to order some food" or "Can I order a takeaway?"
Expected: AI politely explains that phone orders are not available right now and offers to help with a reservation or answer questions instead.
6
Say nothing — silence test
RETRY
What to do
Stay silent for a few seconds after the greeting.
Expected: AI politely prompts you again. After two attempts it thanks you and ends the call gracefully.
7
Say something unexpected
OTHER
Say something like
"What's the weather today?" or "I need to speak to a manager."
Expected: AI acknowledges it can't help with that and offers to assist with reservations, specials, or questions about the restaurant.
What to pay attention to
🎧
Voice quality
Is the AI voice clear and natural? Does it sound like a good restaurant receptionist?
🧠
Understanding
Did it understand what you asked, even with an accent or casual phrasing?
⚡
Response speed
How long did it take to respond after you finished speaking?
💬
Conversation flow
Did the conversation feel natural, or did anything feel awkward or out of place?
